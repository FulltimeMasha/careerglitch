How it works — super simple
  • 1📩 You get a task. A client writes to you. Real numbers. Real mess.
  • 2✍️ You do the work. You write the answer right here. That answer is your artifact — your proof of work.
  • 3💬 You talk it through. The client or manager in the scene is live — ask questions, push back, get pushed. Then you write the work.
  • 4🏆 You get reviewed — and earn proof. Your artifact is checked against a short, public standard: every line true = scene won. A won scene flips the same skill on your Watchdog card, with your artifact as the proof.

Honest words: this is practice work, not a diploma and not a job promise. It is a record of real tasks, done well, that anyone can check.
