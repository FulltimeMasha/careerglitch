Privacy Policy

Career Glitch Watchdog · Last updated: November 2026

What we collect

  • Your email address — the one you give us to receive and restore your card.
  • The profession you ask us to check.
  • Your checklist and personalization answers — which top-1% items you already have, and the few answers you give to make your Score personal.

That is all. We do not ask for a password, and we never see or store your payment card number.

How we use it

  • To generate your AI-Proof Score card and your Top 1% road map.
  • To restore your card and road map when you come back with the same email.
  • To run your Watchdog: watch your profession for new AI tools, grants, jobs and rule changes, update your Score, and send your weekly briefing.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it for advertising.

Payments

Payments are processed by Stripe. When you subscribe, your card details go directly to Stripe — they never touch our servers and we do not store them. We receive only what we need to know that your subscription is active (for example, the email and the reference id attached to your checkout).

Questions or deletion

Want to know what we hold about you, or want it deleted? Email us at byrudnik@gmail.com and we will take care of it.
